# brag-plan.md — Wayfinder launch video

## What it is
Wayfinder is a free, no-signup life planner that turns "I feel lost" into a DAG:
write the future you'd want, work backward through 5/3/1-year goals, and connect
small steps with prerequisite arrows that only unlock when they're ready.
Zero accounts, zero servers — the whole app is static HTML/JS.

## The angle
Windows 98 did everything modern apps do. Wayfinder proves the point with a calm,
real loaner: one honest graph, one share caption, no fake metrics. The punchline
landed because the visual identity is real (yellow-on-ink #1a2a4a buttons,
VT323 headings, 2px hard shadows), so "retro" reads as a design opinion, not a
gimmick. Fake-metrics-free honesty is also the product's actual pitch: the
footer of every video frame says "No signup. Plans stay in your browser."

## Tone
`retro-operating` — deadpan Giant / calendrical lo-fi. States claims flatly from
inside the app's own voice; the humor is in what it *stops* pretending.

## Visual identity
- Colors: #1A0A2E ink, #FFF5DC cream, #FFD84D yellow, #FF85C2 pink grid, #FF3D9A sparkle accent.
- Fonts: VT323 (all headings), Nunito 700 (body), Press Start 2P (tick labels). No smooth shadows anywhere.
- Motion law: only transforms on whole windows; interior content is always already laid out. Cursor crosshair appears only in scenes 3-4.

## Sound
- 17-bit console feel: square-wave pad chord (Am → C → G/F), lo-fi keys, 808-style rimshots on scene cuts.
- SFX: pencil "scribble" for arrow draws, regulatory pencil-cursor click, floppy/DSK "chunk" on scene 6.
- Mix: music -14 LUFS bed; SFX ducks music by 4 dB, tops at -8 dBFS.

## Punchline
"Your plans cannot betray you." — the product is honest (local-first DAG),
the frame is honest (no fake claims), so the single deadpan line works.
